Data & interpretation +
  • 509 total applications; 459 have usable structured problem text.
  • The application/application-year observation is the analytical unit.
  • Primary assignment is used here for legibility; concepts can overlap in the underlying SAE representation.
  • 2021 has partial structured problem-text coverage; later years have complete or near-complete coverage.
  • Cells below 10 observations are suppressed.
  • School displays count founder records rather than applications.
  • Lead-applicant gender is supplied and never inferred.
  • Judging scores are not funding or venture outcomes.
  • Support questions are hypotheses, not measured intervention effects.
  • Problem concepts are learned from demand-side text before evaluation outcomes are attached.
