07 · Data & methodology

From judge rows to a problem atlas

The concept taxonomy is learned from demand-side application text alone. Judging outcomes are attached only after representation learning.

  1. 1

    Audit and reconcile

    Identify application and judge keys, exclude conflicts of interest, reconcile duplicate judge identities, and validate track-specific forms.

  2. 2

    Aggregate to applications

    Calculate application-level Recommendation and dimension means, medians, SDs, and judge counts before any portfolio analysis.

  3. 3

    Construct problem text

    Use customer, stakeholder, context, and unmet-need fields. Do not backfill missing records with solution or pitch text.

  4. 4

    Learn overlapping concepts

    Embed locally and train the pinned HypotheSAEs implementation at M=16 and M=32, K=4, independently of outcomes.

  5. 5

    Validate and overlay

    Review every concept, compare three seeds, then attach trends, judges, founders, schools, geography, and industry.
